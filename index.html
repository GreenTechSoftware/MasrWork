Legal Framework

1. Definitions & Stakeholders

MasrWork: The digital intermediary platform connecting parties in Egypt. Company: The commercial entity posting projects or B2B services. Freelancer: The independent professional executing milestones. Client: The business or individual purchasing services and releasing escrow funds in Egyptian Pounds (EGP).

2. Immutable Milestones & The "No-Rollback" Rule

Projects are mapped into specific hours and sequential milestones. Once a client reviews and clicks "Approve & Release Milestone", that phase becomes 100% final, irreversible, and non-refundable. Clients cannot roll back past approved phases, completely protecting the freelancer's earned effort against refund abuse.

3. Legal Rights of Stakeholders

Companies: Guarantee of proper delivery through AI smart agreements and refunds for un-commenced milestones. Freelancers: Immediate incremental EGP payouts upon milestone approval and protection against retroactive claim fraud. Platform: Authority to ban users violating security or payment protocols.

4. Data Privacy & Compliance (Law No. 151 / 2020)

MasrWork complies with Egyptian data protection laws. Data collection is strictly limited to verification, escrow transactions, and operational security. Commercial or marketing data-sharing with third parties is strictly prohibited.

5. Payments & Escrow Security

All transactions are processed strictly in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) through certified local payment gateways (Paymob, Fawry) under Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) compliance, adhering to anti-money laundering (AML) protocols.

6. Fraud, Identity Misuse & Penalties

Identity forgery, fake profiles, or financial fraud using stolen personal data are treated as criminal offenses. MasrWork will hand over all digital logs, chat histories, and IP addresses to the Egyptian Cybercrime Police and prosecuting authorities for immediate legal action.
