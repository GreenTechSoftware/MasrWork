Executive Summary

MasrWork is a next-generation B2B and freelance platform customized specifically for the Egyptian market. Operating strictly in Egyptian Pounds (EGP), the platform solves core pain points in remote contracting: payment disputes, currency complexities, and lack of legal security. By integrating AI talent matching and immutable milestone approvals, MasrWork bridges businesses and elite Egyptian freelancers safely and efficiently.

Key Strategic Pillars

01

Immutable Milestones

Once a client approves a milestone, it becomes 100% final and non-refundable, completely shielding freelancers from retro-active claim fraud.

02

EGP Escrow Security

Transactions are locked via certified local gateways (Paymob/Fawry) under Central Bank of Egypt regulations, eliminating FX friction.

03

AI-Powered Matching

Smart algorithms connect companies instantly with pre-vetted senior Egyptian developers, designers, and marketers with high match accuracy.

Legal & Data Protection Framework

In strict compliance with Egyptian Law No. 151/2020 on Data Protection, MasrWork maintains rigid privacy measures. Identity forgery, fake profiles, or financial fraud are treated as criminal offenses; all digital logs and IP records are coordinated with Egyptian Cybercrime authorities when necessary to guarantee absolute platform integrity.
